Welcome to Cosmic Club! This is an interactive canvas for web space explorers. Join the space canvas and let your custom artwork float around in space!

To place your website in orbit, create your own space symbol and submit your information using the dedicated Google Form!

Requirements & Requested Data:

  • Site Name: The display name of your site (without the .neocities.org suffix).
  • Website URL: The direct link to your personal website or blog.
  • Custom Symbol / Artwork: A space-themed icon (e.g., planet, star, comet, rocket, UFO, astronaut, alien, ...).
    • Dimensions: 200x200 pixels
    • Background: Transparent (PNG or GIF format)
  • Artwork Upload & Link: Please attach your image file directly in the form AND provide the direct URL of the image hosted on your site.
  • Tags: A few keywords to describe your site or style, separated by commas (e.g., blog, art, games,...).
  • 88x31 Button: The HTML code for your 88x31 button (e.g., <a href="..."><img src="..." /></a>) OR simply the direct URL of your 88x31 button image.
